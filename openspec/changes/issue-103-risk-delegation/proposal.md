# Risk-bound continuation for #103

## Why

The existing FF/hotfix contract requires independent consideration of risk, reversibility, scope and evidence. Its eligibility flags do not retain a structured explanation of affected surfaces or compare a candidate with exact files assessed at refinement.

## What Changes

Add an optional restrictive assessment to the existing unattended policy. The trusted host records separate inherent-risk, unresolved-defect and evidence-confidence observations, applies project sensitive-path stops, checks actual changed files against admitted scope, and retains bound decisions at admission, candidate assessment and publication. Existing checks, independent reviews, correction limits and separate merge authority remain mandatory.

## Product alignment

FR-FAST-FORWARD-DELIVERY owns bounded low-risk continuation and fresh scope/evidence. BR-ENTRY-AUTHORITY separates authorization from autonomy; BR-HUMAN-AUTHORITY retains material owner decisions. These current artifacts already cover the requested behavior: this implements their existing limits without changing product meaning or increasing delegation, so no new Product Change is needed.
