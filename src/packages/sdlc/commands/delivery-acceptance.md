# Delivery acceptance

Read the [source and adoption contract](../delivery-acceptance.md). Prepare the exact binding with `lighthouse-sdlc delivery-acceptance prepare`; the repository owner authors the resulting decision comment. Record it through the adopted non-agent workflow, then run `verify` with independently readable sources and all retained receipts for that binding.

Report the observed status and source limitations. Product approval, engineering results and lifecycle events retain their existing meanings. Acceptance grants no mutation authority. An undeclared route, expired source or incomplete correction history is unavailable; a healthy route without a decision is pending.
