## Purpose

Supply or consume an adopted authority such as product definition, architecture documentation,
factory operation or agent workflow.

## Goals

Exchange only the context and managed surfaces required by an explicit Lighthouse integration.

## Responsibilities

Validate its own owned artifacts and report compatibility or drift at its boundary.

## Boundaries

An integrated system remains authoritative for its own domain and does not silently transfer
ownership of its files or decisions to Lighthouse.
