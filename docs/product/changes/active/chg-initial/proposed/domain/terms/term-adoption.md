## Definition

The controlled act of applying a Lighthouse foundation or profile to an existing or new project,
recording what is enabled and verifying the resulting project state.

## Distinguish From

Adoption is not a bulk overwrite or an assertion that a project has accepted every recommended
practice.

## Usage

The adoption route describes prerequisites, owned files, checks, exceptions and evidence for a
project.
