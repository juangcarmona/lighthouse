## Definition

The explicit mapping that identifies which system owns product intent, architecture evidence, delivery contracts and project decisions for a change.

## Distinguish From

An authority map is not a mandatory serial pipeline or a transfer of ownership between integrations.

## Usage

ProductShape owns product intent, ArcShape owns architecture evidence, and Lighthouse owns the delivery harness and its evidence while each authority can advance at its own speed.
