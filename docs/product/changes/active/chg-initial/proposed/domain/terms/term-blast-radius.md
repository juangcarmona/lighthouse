## Definition

Blast radius is the estimated scope and consequence of an incorrect or incomplete change across components, users, data boundaries, repositories or environments.

## Distinguish From

Blast radius describes what can be affected if the change is wrong; complexity describes the effort and uncertainty involved in implementing it.

## Usage

Lighthouse records a coarse blast-radius level with confidence and names the affected boundaries that justify it.
