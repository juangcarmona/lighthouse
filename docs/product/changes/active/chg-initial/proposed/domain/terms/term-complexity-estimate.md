## Definition

A complexity estimate is a coarse, confidence-bearing assessment of scope, dependencies, uncertainty and verification effort for a work item.

## Distinguish From

An estimate is not a blast-radius assessment, a deadline, a promise of delivery or a substitute for refinement.

## Usage

Lighthouse records a small fixed scale such as `S`, `M`, `L` or `XL` and requires re-estimation when scope, dependencies or evidence change materially.
