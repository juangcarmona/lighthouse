## Definition

A project file generated or maintained from an integration contract whose ownership, update rules
and drift checks are explicit.

## Distinguish From

A managed projection is not an unreviewed copy of another repository's source and cannot silently
replace project-owned content.

## Usage

Lighthouse may maintain a small project guidance projection while ProductShape, ArcShape and other
integrations retain ownership of their own generated surfaces.
