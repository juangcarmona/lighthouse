## Constraint

Lighthouse MUST integrate with established ProductShape, ArcShape, OpenSpec, SDLC package, factory and agent workflow capabilities where they already satisfy the need, and MUST require evidence before adding a duplicate engine or provider.

## Rationale

The foundation centralizes the project experience while avoiding parallel sources of lifecycle,
product or architecture truth.

## Consequences

New Lighthouse functionality needs a clear gap, an ownership decision and a representative adoption
case before it becomes part of the baseline.
