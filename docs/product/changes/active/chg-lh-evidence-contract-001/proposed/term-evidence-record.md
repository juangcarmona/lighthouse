## Definition

A versioned, producer-owned fact describing an observed result, its scope and status, its provenance, and the optional product, architecture or delivery subjects and artifacts that it supports.

## Distinguish From

An evidence record is not a product requirement, an architecture decision, a quality score or an agent transcript. It does not change the authority that it references.

## Usage

Stage results, browser journeys, guardrail checks and provider operations can be adapted into the common evidence record without losing their original payload or ownership.
