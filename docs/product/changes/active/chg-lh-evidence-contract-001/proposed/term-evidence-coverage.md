## Definition

A deterministic projection that relates evidence records to declared product, architecture or delivery subjects and reports the observed coverage state without changing the subject authority.

## Distinguish From

Evidence coverage is not a claim that a subject is correct, complete or valuable, and it is not a quality score. It reports the evidence available for review.

## Usage

Lighthouse can project ProductShape requirements and ArcShape decisions as unverified, observed, verified, incomplete, conflicting or not applicable when the corresponding evidence records are available.
