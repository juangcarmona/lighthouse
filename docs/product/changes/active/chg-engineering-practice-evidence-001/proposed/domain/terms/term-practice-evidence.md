## Definition

A versioned observation of one declared engineering practice at an identified repository revision and source run. It records the practice definition and mode, applicability, source result, collection time and coverage needed to audit the observation.

## Distinguish From

A guardrail sets an expectation and failure consequence. Practice evidence reports what an identified source observed; it does not make that expectation binding, assign a score or prove other practices passed.

## Usage

An adopter publishes practice evidence from its own verification. A read-only consumer preserves the source identity and unknown states when showing it.
