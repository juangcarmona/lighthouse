## Definition

Work-item size is a coarse assessment of the volume and scope envelope of a work item across its affected boundaries and verification work.

## Distinguish From

Size is not complexity, elapsed time, urgency, risk or a delivery promise.

## Usage

Lighthouse records one value from `S`, `M`, `L` or `XL` and re-estimates it when scope, dependencies or evidence change materially.
