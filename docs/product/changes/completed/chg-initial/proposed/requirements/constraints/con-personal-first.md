## Constraint

The initial foundation MUST optimize for use across Juan's own repositories and MUST NOT require a
hosted multi-tenant service, organization identity platform or commercial support operation.

## Rationale

The first product risk is whether the maintainer can repeatedly ship with the foundation, not whether
it can serve an external customer base.

## Consequences

Local installation, repository-owned configuration and focused profiles are preferred until repeated
adoption proves a broader distribution contract.
