## Definition

A public site projection is a generated, reader-oriented view of Lighthouse product intent, lifecycle contracts and architecture evidence.

## Distinguish From

The projection is not a second product definition or architecture source, and its presentation copy does not override the authorities that feed it.

## Usage

The site combines ProductShape for what and why, ArcShape for how, and selected workflow diagrams for the public explanation of adoption and delivery.
