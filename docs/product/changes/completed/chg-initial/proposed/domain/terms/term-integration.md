## Definition

A declared boundary through which Lighthouse exchanges configuration, guidance or verification with
an authority or tool that remains responsible for its own domain.

## Distinguish From

An integration is not a fork of the connected tool and does not make Lighthouse the owner of its
schemas, lifecycle or generated files.

## Usage

Integrations connect Lighthouse with ProductShape, ArcShape, adopting systems and agent workflow hosts.
