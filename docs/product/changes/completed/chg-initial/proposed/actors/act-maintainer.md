## Purpose

Maintain the shared foundation and decide which conventions are reusable across projects.

## Goals

Keep the foundation small, useful, verifiable and suitable for Juan's projects first.

## Responsibilities

Define the baseline, review changes to managed content, accept exceptions and decide when an
integration or profile is mature enough to support.

## Boundaries

The maintainer does not use Lighthouse to approve product scope, architecture decisions or merges
on behalf of an adopting project.
