## Definition

Work-item triage is the bounded intake assessment that classifies an issue, records urgency, impact, dependencies and risk, estimates complexity and blast radius, and selects the next delivery route.

## Distinguish From

Triage does not design the solution, approve product intent or replace architecture analysis.

## Usage

Every issue entering a Lighthouse delivery route receives a triage result or an explicit decision that triage is out of scope.
