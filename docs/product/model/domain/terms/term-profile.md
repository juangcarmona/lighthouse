## Definition

A bounded selection of stack guidance, starter content, tools and checks for a project capability such as the visual contract or a .NET backend profile.

## Distinguish From

A profile is not a universal requirement and does not determine a project's product or complete
architecture.

## Usage

An adopter selects profiles during initialization or adoption and records the selection in the project's foundation manifest. A full-stack project composes profiles instead of adopting a combined Lighthouse runtime.
