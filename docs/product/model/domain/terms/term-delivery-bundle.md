## Definition

The set of outcomes an adopting repository receives from Lighthouse: its product code remains project-owned, while conventions, the versioned harness, executable workflows and health evidence make delivery repeatable and reviewable.

## Distinguish From

A delivery bundle is the product contract exposed by adoption. It is not a hosted service, an application framework, a replacement for ProductShape or ArcShape, or a promise that verification proves the application is correct.

## Usage

Lighthouse uses the term when describing what an adopter can expect from the baseline, its packages, its workflow package and its deterministic checks.
