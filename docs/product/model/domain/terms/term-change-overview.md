## Definition

A concise human-readable explanation of a change's problem, intended outcome, scope, approach, affected authorities, risks, evidence and next decision.

## Distinguish From

A change overview is not the full proposal, design, task list or delta specification, and it does not approve the change.

## Usage

`propose` produces the change overview after the solution is understood; its prose uses the human-facing writing policy while preserving facts, links, commands and frontmatter.
