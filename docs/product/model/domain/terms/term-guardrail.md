## Definition

An explicit engineering expectation with a stated owner, verification method, failure consequence
and documented exception path.

## Distinguish From

Guidance may explain a recommended practice; a guardrail has an executable or reviewable check and
a known consequence when it is not met.

## Usage

Profiles and the baseline expose guardrails to both adopters and agent hosts.
